What the Delivery Drone System must do

Seven use cases define the service: one core delivery flow plus the capabilities that make it safe and scalable.

1 · Deliver parcels

The operator books a customer's parcel and dispatches a drone. It flies to the landing zone, drops the parcel off and returns home.

Value: faster, cheaper last-mile delivery to remote places than ground transport.

DeliveryProcess · BookDelivery · DeliverPackage

2 · Extend range

A drone with low battery meets a charger drone in flight and recharges without returning to a ground dock.

Value: reach destinations beyond a single battery charge. This is the key differentiator.

ChargeDrone

3 · Operate safely at scale

Drones watch their own health continuously and land safely on a critical fault. One operator sees the status of the whole fleet at once.

Value: protects people, parcels and drones; few operators can run many drones.

MonitorDroneHealth · HandleEmergencyLanding · ReportFleetStatus

Decisions this enables

  • In-flight charging carries the business case for remote delivery. Fund an early feasibility test before committing to remote markets.
  • Safety use cases are not optional. Plan for them in scope and budget from day one, because they are the basis for approval and trust.
  • Drones per operator is the main operating-cost lever. Set a target ratio early, as it drives the fleet-status and automation requirements.
