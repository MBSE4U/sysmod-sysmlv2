# Change Log

## Unreleased (draft)

Two additions for projects with sub-projects and for architecture decisions: the sub-project contract and trade studies. The new step names follow the verb terminology of v5.1.8.

- Sub-project contract: a sub-project is now formally linked to the architecture of its parent project.
  - Sub-project contract, documented at `Project::subProjects` and carried by a separate handover package: a handover context (the black box of the building block that the sub-project develops as the system of interest; its neighbors and the actors delegated to it as actors; its connections and delegations as actor/system interfaces), the handed-over stakeholders, and the handover requirements. The parent white box types the building block by the system of interest definition and specializes the other elements; the sub-project uses the handover context as its black box brownfield context, so the sub-project, e.g., a supplier, needs no access to the parent project. The parent integrates the product system of the sub-project by binding the building block to it, or keeps the black box if a supplier delivers no white box.
  - New `Project::requirementFlowDowns` (`abstract connection`, `DerivationConnections::Derivation [0..*]`, subsets `DerivationConnections::derivations`): the `#derivation` connections from the requirements of a project (`#original`) to the `handoverRequirements` of its sub-projects (`#derive`). They are declared in the parent project, because SysML v2 only allows connection ends that are accessible from the owner of the connection; a sub-project cannot refer to a requirement nested in a parent definition.
  - New `Project::handoverRequirements` (`requirement`, `ExtendedRequirement [0..1]`): for a sub-project, the requirements that the parent hands over, typed by a requirement definition of the handover package. The parent derives them from its requirements (`requirementFlowDowns`); the sub-project derives its system requirements from them with `#derivation` connections. With the handover stakeholders, the sub-project needs no access to the parent model.
  - New steps `ModelSubProjects` (verb level model; outputs `subProjects` and `requirementFlowDowns`; refines System Architecture Definition) and `IntegrateSubProjects` (outside the verb scale, like `PerformVerification`; `inout productContextImpl`; refines Integration). `SYSMODDefaultProcess` performs them between `modelSolutionArchitecture` and `specifyVerificationContext`; between them, each sub-project performs its own process. The verb table lists `ModelSubProjects` (model) and names integrate as an activity verb outside the scale.
  - Doc of `subProjects` and `SpecifyVerificationContext` aligned (created by `modelSubProjects`; the integrated product system can be the system of interest of the verification context).
- Trade studies for architecture decisions.
  - New `SYSMODTradeStudy` analysis def specializing `TradeStudies::TradeStudy`: subject `alternatives : SystemContext [1..*]`, `criteria` (system objectives), `assessments : Assessment [0..*]`, the `evaluationFunction` as the weighted sum of the assessments of an alternative, a `MaximizeObjective`, and the typed `selectedAlternative`.
  - New attribute def `Assessment`: `alternative`, `criterion`, `weight` (default: the `priority` of the system objective), `score` (degree of achievement, asserted to lie between 0.0 and 1.0), and `weightedScore`.
  - New `Project::tradeStudies : SYSMODTradeStudy [0..*]`. The alternatives are the variants of the project context of the decision, which becomes a variation usage (e.g., `variation part :>> productContextImpl`). The variants are declared inside the variation, which records the decision with `ModelingMetadata::Rationale` whose `explanation` is the trade study. The trade study refers to the variants via reference features of the project (`ref part <alternative> = <variation>::<variant>`), because SysML v2 Pilot Implementation 0.63.1 rejects references to variants from an analysis. The pilot also reports annotations owned by a variation as "A package-level usage must be referential"; the specification allows them.
  - New step `SpecifyTradeStudy` (verb level specify, `[0..*]`, refines Decision Management and System Analysis). It has no succession: a project performs it for any architecture decision with alternatives, e.g., between candidate system ideas or candidate product architectures. It takes the open project context of the decision as `in decisionContext` and returns it as `out decidedContext`: a variation usage whose variants are the alternatives, bound to the selected variant (`= <variation>::<selected variant>`), with `@Rationale`. Separate parameters instead of one `inout` parameter make the change of the context visible: before the decision a plain context, after it the decided variation.
- System objectives with measures of effectiveness (MOE) and measures of performance (MOP).
  - New concern def `SystemObjective :> ExtendedConcern` with the attributes `measuresOfEffectiveness` (`#ParametersOfInterestMetadata::MeasureOfEffectiveness`) and `measuresOfPerformance` (`#ParametersOfInterestMetadata::MeasureOfPerformance`), each `[*] nonunique`. The measures of an objective subset them; the target values are stated as required constraints on the measures.
  - New step `DefineSystemObjectives` (verb level define, the first step at this level; refines Business or Mission Analysis). It takes the `problemStatement`, the `stakeholderNeeds`, and the `systemIdeaContextImpl` and outputs the `systemObjectives`, which `DescribeSystemIdea` no longer outputs. Updated the doc of `DescribeSystemIdea` and `SYSMODDefaultProcess`, the verb table, the `#refinement` dependencies, `defaultProcessAI` (`systemObjectivesAI` belongs to the new step), the SYSMOD Map cell A2, `OnlyProcessStepElements`, and `README.md`.
  - `Project::systemObjectives` and the `systemObjectives` parameters of `DefineSystemObjectives`, `SpecifySystem`, `specifySystemRequirements`, and `SpecifyTradeStudy` are typed by `SystemObjective` instead of `ExtendedConcern`.
  - `systemObjectivesAI` creates and validates the MOEs and MOPs of the objectives.
  - Delivery Drone: `FastRemoteDelivery`, `LowCostPerDelivery`, and `OnTimeDelivery` specialize `SystemObjective` with the MOEs `deliveryTimeRatio`, `costRatio`, and `onTimeRate`, the MOPs `airChargingTime` and `airChargingSuccessRate`, and required constraints against their target values.
- Methodology metamodel: the three new steps `ModelSubProjects`, `IntegrateSubProjects`, and `SpecifyTradeStudy` specialize `MBSEMethodology::Method`, and the system architect is responsible for them (`responsibleFor` connections in `SYSMODDefaultProcess`, like `modelBrownfieldSystem`).
  - Parameters that can also be a `Product` (an item) subset `inputs` or `outputs`: `productContextImpl`, `subProjects`, and the `decisionContext` and `decidedContext` of `SpecifyTradeStudy`. Requirements, concerns, and analyses cannot, because they are performances and `Performance` is disjoint from `Object`; the `requirementFlowDowns` cannot either, because a connection usage may only be typed by connection definitions.
  - `ModelSubProjects` and `IntegrateSubProjects` require at least one sub-project (`subProjects [1..*]`), so `ModelSubProjects` always has an output (`outputs [1..*]`).
- EEE Check (`EEE.sysml`, new library package `EEE`): an extension of `MBSEMethodology` for the EEE Check (Engineering Efficiency Empowerment) by oose and Willert, anchored at the MBSE environment.
  - `EeeCheck` (`#eeeCheck`) with `environment : MBSEEnvironment [1]`: the analyzed environment; its roles and tools are the rows and columns of the EEE Tools Roles Methods Matrix.
  - `ProcessStep :> MethodologyBehavior` (`#processStep`): `role :> primaryPerformer`, `tool :> facilitationTools`, `method : Method`, `sourceArtifacts :> inputs`, `targetArtifacts :> outputs`, and the swimlanes `goal`, `viewpoints`, `automationSteps`, `artifactConversions` of the EEE Process Step Definition Diagram.
  - `StepTransition` (`#stepTransition`, connection between two process steps) with `evaluation : TransitionEvaluation` (`unrated`, `efficient`, `bottleneck`), `priority`, and the derived `isToolBreak` and `isHumanInterface`; `Measure` (`#measure`) with `addressedTransitions`.
  - `ToolPolicy` (`#toolPolicy`) with `environment : MBSEEnvironment [1]` and `ToolPolicyRule` (`#toolPolicyRule`): `tool`, `method`, `status : PolicyStatus` (`recommended`, `allowed`, `forbidden`).
  - New example `examples/EEECheckControlUnit.sysml`: a company environment with the tools and the methods of its methodology, a team environment that specializes it with roles and products, the EEE check of the team environment, and the company tool policy.
- MBSE Methodology editor (`editor/`): web-based editor for MBSE environments and their EEE checks (environment, Tools Roles Methods Matrix, Process Step Definition Diagram, Tool Policy Matrix, forms, text), based on the PLEML editor; standalone file `editor/dist/mbse-methodology-editor.html`.
- Views (`SYSMODViews.sysml`):
  - New `SpecificationTreeViewpoint` (frames `ProblemSolutionIntegration` and `ContractualAgreement`) and `SpecificationTreeView`.
  - New `TradeStudyView`, a decision matrix, and the first view for the `TradeOffViewpoint`. The `TradeOffViewpoint` now allows `ExtendedConcern` (the system objectives as criteria) instead of `ExtendedRequirement` only.
  - `OnlyProcessStepElements` includes the three new steps; the doc of the `asSYSMODMap` rendering assigns `subProjects` to `modelSubProjects` and names the steps without a cell of their own.
  - `#viewedArtifact` dependencies for the new views.
- AI metadata (`SYSMOD4AI.sysml`):
  - New `tradeStudiesAI` (`sysmod-trade-studies`) and `handoverAI` (`sysmod-sub-project-contract`, about `handoverRequirements`), the sub-project side of the contract: take over the handover context as the brownfield context, derive the system requirements, and propose the flow-down. `subProjectsAI` creates the handover packages on the parent side.
  - `subProjectsAI` covers the parent side of the contract: creating the handover packages, declaring the flow-down, and integrating the sub-projects.

## v5.1.8

- Split verification into define and bind, so that verification engineers enter the model while the system is specified and discuss the verification with the systems engineers in SysML instead of in a separate test language. Previously, all verification happened in the last step, after the product architecture.
  - The verification cases stay in the `verificationContext` (`verificationCases`); there is no separate Project feature for the verification cases of the specification. A verification case is the same case before and after the product architecture exists: its definition has the black box specification system as subject, and its usage in the verification context binds the subject to the system under verification.
  - Define: new nested step `specifyVerificationCases` of `SpecifySystem`. It specifies the verification case definitions with objective (`verify`) and verdict (`PassIf`) against the black box specification system and their usages in the verification context, and returns the updated `verificationContext` (new output of `SpecifySystem`, flowing to the project); the `VerificationEngineer` is a stakeholder of this step. `SYSMODDefaultProcess` therefore creates the verification cases before `modelFunctionalArchitecture`. `SpecifySystem` additionally refines the ISO 15288 Verification process (preparation for verification). Added `specifyVerificationCases` to the verb table (level specify).
  - Bind: `SpecifyVerificationContext` takes the `verificationContext` as `inout` parameter (bound to the project), adds the test systems, and binds the verification cases to the system under verification; it no longer creates verification cases anew.
  - Delivery Drone: `MaxPayloadTest` moved from `DD_VV` (product architecture) to the new package `DD_SPECIFICATION::Verification`, with the black box `DeliveryDroneSystem` as subject. Its usage `maxPayloadTest` in `deliveryDroneVerificationContext` binds it to `DeliveryDroneProductSystemImpl`, including the drone under test (`maxReq` subject `:> productSystem.drones`).
  - Delivery Drone: new project stakeholder `flightTestTeam` ("Flight Test Team") playing the `VerificationEngineer` role (risk middle, effort middle, category expert); it specifies `maxPayloadTest` with the systems engineers. Updated the example views `stakeholdersView.svg` and `stakeholderNeedsView.svg`.
  - `SYSMOD4AI.sysml`: new AI metadata `verificationCasesAI` (sysmod-verification-cases, about `verificationContext::verificationCases`) and `verificationContextAI` (sysmod-verification-context); updated the process prompts of `defaultProcessAI` and the project prompts of `projectAI`.
  - `SYSMODViews.sysml`: the SYSMOD Map shows `verificationContext.verificationCases` in A3 (`specifySystem`) and A7 (`specifyVerificationContext`); `VerificationMatrixView` reviews the verification coverage during the specification; updated the `VerificationViewpoint` doc. Updated the example views `sysmodMapView.svg` and `verificationMatrixView.svg`, `README.md`, and the regenerated `SYSMODViews.md`.
  - Models that typed the subject of their verification case definitions with a product architecture definition should move the definition to the specification, type the subject with the black box specification system, and keep the usage in the verification context.
- Refactored the SYSMOD process:
  - The steps `ModelFunctionalArchitecture`, `ModelLogicalArchitecture`, and `ModelProductArchitecture` are merged into the new step `ModelSolutionArchitecture` (`modelSolutionArchitecture`) with the nested steps `modelFunctionalArchitecture` (optional), `modelLogicalArchitecture` (optional), and `modelProductArchitecture` (optional), in this order. The functional and logical contexts flow between the nested steps; the nested steps also read the `systemRequirementSpecification`. `ModelSolutionArchitecture` refines the ISO 15288 System Architecture Definition process.
  - The product architecture is optional like the functional and logical architectures: the project features `productContext` and `productContextImpl` now have the multiplicity `[0..1]`.
  - The verification context is open: `verificationContext` is typed by `SystemContext` instead of specializing `productContextImpl`, so its system of interest can be any system under verification, e.g., the product system, a prototype, or a simulation model. The library subject `productSystem` of `verificationCases` is removed, and `SpecifyVerificationContext` no longer takes `productContextImpl` as input. Models must remove `productSystem` from their verification case subject redefinitions (`subject :>> testedSystem : …;` instead of `subject :>> productSystem, testedSystem : …;`). Updated the prompts of `productContextAI`, `defaultProcessAI`, `verificationCasesAI`, and `verificationContextAI`, the verification view docs, the Delivery Drone project, and the MicroStory snippets `verification` and `validation`.
  - The nested steps carry `@StepStatus`; a project sets their status inside `action :>> modelSolutionArchitecture { … }`. `scripts/generate_map_status.py` reads nested steps (new field `parent` in `sysmodMapStatus.json`), and the SYSMOD Map shows the status of the nested step in the columns 4 to 6.
  - The process inputs are renamed to `brownfieldArchitectureDescription`, `problemStatementDescription` (now an item typed by the new item definition `ProblemStatementDescription` instead of a string attribute), `systemIdeaArchitectureDescription`, `functionalArchitectureDescription`, `logicalArchitectureDescription`, and `productArchitectureDescription`. They flow into the inputs of the steps instead of being bound in the step usages.
  - Updated the `OnlyProcessStepElements` constraint (now also with `PerformVerification`) and the SYSMOD Map docs of `SYSMODViews.sysml`, the process and architecture prompts of `SYSMOD4AI.sysml`, `README.md`, and the Delivery Drone `@StepStatus` redefinitions. `scripts/generate_views_doc.py` reads the outputs of nested steps bound with `bind` and the flows between nested steps.
  - Models that redefine `modelFunctionalArchitecture`, `modelLogicalArchitecture`, or `modelProductArchitecture` in their `defaultProcess` must nest them in `modelSolutionArchitecture`.
- Specification context: subparts or attributes of the system that use cases and requirements refer to belong to the white box `specificationContextImpl`, not to the black box `specificationContext`, which contains the actors and the system at the system boundary. Updated the docs of `specificationContext`, `specificationContextImpl`, and the nested step `modelSpecificationContext`, and the create prompt, question, and validation of `specificationContextAI`.
  - The subject of `systemRequirementSpecification` is now the white box specification system: `subject specificationSystemImpl :> specificationContextImpl.soiImpl` (before: `specificationSystem :> specificationContext.soi`), so that nested requirements can refer to subparts of the system. The use cases keep the black box `specificationSystem` as subject. Models that redefine the requirement subject must use `specificationSystemImpl` (`subject :>> specificationSystemImpl, …`). Updated the prompts of `systemRequirementSpecificationAI`.
  - Delivery Drone: the root requirement `DeliveryDroneSystemRequirements` has the white box `DeliveryDroneSystemImpl` as subject and binds the subject of `maxPayloadMass` to its drones (`subject :>> deliveryDrone :> deliveryDroneSystem.drones`); the project's requirement subject subsets `deliveryDroneSpecificationContextImpl.deliveryDroneSystemImpl`. Updated the MicroStory snippets `system-requirements`, `requirements-traceability`, and `the-full-chain`.
- New step `performVerification` (`PerformVerification`) after `specifyVerificationContext`: it executes the verification cases of the verification context on the product system and documents the results as verification reports. It refines the ISO 15288 Verification process.
  - New item definition `VerificationReport`: Verification Report — supports the Verification process of ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288; content aligned with ISO/IEC/IEEE 15289. The dependency `verificationReportSupportsVerification` links it to `ISO15288Processes::Verification`.
  - New item usage `verificationContext.verificationReports[*]`; the output `verificationReports` of `performVerification` flows into it, and the step reads the `verificationContext` of the project.
  - The verb table of the `SYSMODProcesses` package doc lists perform (execute specified content, e.g., the verification cases) as an activity verb outside the scale; `PerformVerification` is outside the scale like `SetupProject`.
  - SYSMOD Map: B7 to C7 also shows the `verificationReports` (`performVerification`) and, as two steps create the cell's features, the status of each step. Updated the `SYSMODMapView` and `asSYSMODMap` docs and the example view `sysmodMapView.svg`.
  - Updated the process prompts of `defaultProcessAI` in `SYSMOD4AI.sysml` and `README.md`. The Delivery Drone project sets the status of `performVerification` (open); regenerated `SYSMODViews.md` and `examples/views/sysmodMapStatus.json`.
  - `scripts/generate_views_doc.py` handles `inout` step parameters: a later step that updates an artifact, e.g., `SpecifyVerificationContext` the `verificationContext`, is listed as an additional creating step.
- Renamed the SYSMOD steps to follow a verb terminology: step names are verb + product, and the verb states how formal the content of the step's output is, from informal to fully formal: describe, identify, define, model, specify. A step with nested steps takes the verb of its most formal nested step. The terminology is documented as a table (level, verb, result, test question, steps) in the `SYSMODProcesses` package doc.
  - `SpecifyBrownfieldSystem` → `ModelBrownfieldSystem` (`modelBrownfieldSystem`)
  - `SpecifyProblemStatement` → `DescribeProblemStatement` (`describeProblemStatement`)
  - `ElaborateStakeholderNeeds` → `IdentifyStakeholderNeeds` (`identifyStakeholderNeeds`)
  - `PitchSystemIdea` → `DescribeSystemIdea` (`describeSystemIdea`)
  - `DefineSystemSpecification` → `SpecifySystem` (`specifySystem`), with the nested steps `specifySpecificationArchitecture` → `modelSpecificationContext` and `elaborateSystemRequirements` → `specifySystemRequirements`; `identifyUseCases` is unchanged
  - `CreateFunctionalArchitecture`, `CreateLogicalArchitecture`, `CreateProductArchitecture` → `ModelFunctionalArchitecture`, `ModelLogicalArchitecture`, `ModelProductArchitecture` (`model…Architecture`)
  - `VerifyAndValidateRequirements` → `SpecifyVerificationContext` (`specifyVerificationContext`)
  - `SetupProject` and `IdentifyStakeholders` are unchanged.
  - The step doc comments use the new verbs. Updated the references in `SYSMOD4AI.sysml` (process prompts), `SYSMODViews.sysml` (`OnlyProcessStepElements` constraint, SYSMOD Map docs), `README.md`, the Delivery Drone project (`@StepStatus` redefinitions), and the docstrings of the generator scripts; regenerated `SYSMODViews.md` and `examples/views/sysmodMapStatus.json`.
  - Models that redefine steps of `defaultProcess` must use the new names.
